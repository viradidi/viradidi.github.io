About

Data engineering across cloud and real-time platforms, with a statistician's habits.

I am a data engineer working with cloud data platforms, analytics engineering, lakehouse design and real-time streaming. I hold a Bachelor of Science in Biostatistics from Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT). My statistical training shapes how I build: define the question, understand how the data was generated, and test assumptions before relying on results.

My projects are portfolio projects. I keep a clear line between what I have run and what evidence the repository retains.

Engineering principles

Reliability and idempotency

A rerun should change nothing or stop and say why.

Metadata-driven design

Load behaviour belongs in configuration where that is practical.

Data quality and validation

Check data where it enters and where it changes shape.

Observability

Every stage leaves an audit trail someone can read.

Reproducibility and automation

Another engineer should be able to rebuild the platform from the repository.

Tools chosen on requirements

Python, SQL, Spark or Flink SQL, depending on the problem.

Building reliable data platforms

I start with the business question, pick an architecture that fits it, validate at the boundaries, make reruns safe and write down the trade-offs. Each project page lists its engineering decisions and its limits.
